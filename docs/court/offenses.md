# Offenses
This page covers the general guidelines to creating an offense in the server.

When making an offense, you don't need to specify the extremity of the charge. Whether that be it's a misdeameanor or a felony the Court and its members will use it appropriately to their own interpretation.

!!! note "Difference to Sueable Offenses"
    
    With sueable offenses, one party benefits from winning the trial. In a criminal offense trial it's either the defendant gets punished or they walk free. It will typically result in more ruthless punishments.


You can make the criminal offense anything you desire so long as it abides by the server's rules. 

!!! example "Examples"
    * Someone is commiting a crime when they say the word, `PLACEHOLDER`.
    * If anyone does `PLACEHOLDER` they are commiting a crime.
    * Someone is commiting a crime if `PLACEHOLDER_1` and `PLACEHOLDER_2` happen within ten minutes of each other.

    <span class="transparent"><i>Note: These examples are pretty boring. Not every criminal offense needs to be crazy or wacky but have fun and be creative with what you submit! It makes court more enticing and fun to play. Thank you!</i></span>
