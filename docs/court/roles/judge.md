# Judge
Your purpose is to ensure the trial goes smoothly. This page will serve as an all-in-one guide on how you can be a judge in Tucker Isles's Court party game!

## Court Intermission
During the Court's intermission is the best time to appoint your assistants for the trial.

`What assistants are good to have?`
:   1. Time Keeper
    :   It's important to have someone keeping track of the time. This person needs to ensure that no round exceeds ten minutes long. If it does they need to alert the judge that the round's time has expired.
    2. Peace Keeper
    :   This person will yell at anyone that disrupts the fluency of the trial. They have the ability to mute any one person for three minutes every round.

`Don't assistants seem like actual roles?`
:   They are official roles but are non-essential to the trial and instead are additive to spice up the trial.

With the rest of the time you have in intermission, keep a close eye on your DMs. It's not against the rules for any party of a trial to DM you in private.

## Court Play Time
You are the officiater for every round, and no round can proceed without your spoken or written initiation.

You are especially expected to listen for the majority of the trial. However you do bear spoken authority to a certain degree. In other words, you can inquire to the party that's arguing for that round <span class="transparent">(prioritized party)</span>, and if the opposing party says a callout like, <span class="transparent">(e.g.)</span> "heresay" you can object to it and let the prioritized party continue.
