# Prosecutor
You're either a criminal prosecutor or someone suing another person. Either way your task it to punish the defendant for the alledged acts they've committed.

## Court Intermission
If you haven't already organized your thoughts and materials for the trial beforehand. The intermission is your time to do so. Regardless of if you're alone or accompanied by lawyer<span class="transparent">(s)</span> you need a game plan, evidence, and preperation. 

`How should I interact with my lawyers?`
:   However you want, obviously. But generally you should treat them well since your ability to win is dependent on them. Ensure that you're working with your lawyer<span class="transparent">(s)</span> than against. 

## Court Play Time
Don't say anything that might weaken your argument. Rely on your lawyer<span class="transparent">(s)</span> as they're there to present you.
