# Lawyer
Whether you're representing a prosecutor or a defendant you're going to be arguing something. Your responsibility is to work with your client throughout the intermission and play time to ensure that your party wins.

## Tips and Tricks

`How to prepare evidence?`
:   Using a slideshow format is the ideal way to present evidence in Court. Most people will organize a quick Google slideshow for their evidence and present it.

`When can I edit my evidence presentation?`
:   Anytime! You are allowed to adjust and tweak your evidence presentation whenever needed.

`How do I talk to my party when the other party is presenting?`
:   I recommend creating a small group chat with your client and other lawyers. That way at any point during the trial you can reach out to them without interrupting or obstructing the proceeding of the trial.
