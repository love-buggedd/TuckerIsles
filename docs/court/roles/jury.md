# Jury
The jury consists of all the spectators during the trial. The role of the jury is to pay attention to the trial, provide additional inquiries, and assist in determining the outcome of the trial.

## Unique Sub-Roles
Provided the trial's judge decides they want assistant<span class="transparent">(s)</span>, members of the jury can be selected for roles that increase the unpredictability and chaos of the trial.
