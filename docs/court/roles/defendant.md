# Defendant
You've been sued or have committed a crime. Either way the defendant is the target of attention for any trial. Your role is to defend yourself with the help of your lawyer<span class="transparent">(s)</span>.

## Court Intermission
If you haven't already organized your thoughts and materials for the trial beforehand. The intermission is your time to do so. Regardless of if you're alone or accompanied by lawyers you need a game plan, evidence, and preperation. 

`How should I interact with my lawyers?`
:   However you want, obviously. But generally you should treat them well since your ability to win is dependent on them. Ensure that you're working with your lawyers than against. 

## Court Play Time
Don't say anything that might weaken your argument or get you incriminated. Rely on your lawyers as they're there to present you.
