# Suing Guidelines
Suing another person is a way to involve the community more in a trial rather than commiting a server offense. Only one party benefits at the end of it, and any civil trial has the chance to backfire on the prosecutor.

Much like criminal offenses there are no hard guidelines to creating a sueable offense. However, it should follow the server's rules and it should be and offense that encroaches on another person's wellbeing in some way.

!!! example "Examples"
    * Someone can be sued if they cause `PLACEHOLDER_1` to happen as a direct result of their negligence or recklessness with `PLACEHOLDER_2`.
    * If anyone breaks a verbal or written agreement with `PLACEHOLDER`, they can be sued.
    * Someone can be sued if they make false or damaging statements about another person that harm their reputation.

    <span class="transparent"><i>Note: These examples are pretty boring. Not every sueable offense needs to be crazy or wacky but have fun and be creative with what you submit! It makes court more enticing and fun to play. Thank you!</i></span>

!!! danger "Disclaimer"
    Any sueable offense shouldn't specify a person in it. Instead it should be able to apply to any two or more people.
