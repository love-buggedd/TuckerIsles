# Charges
!!! info inline "User Generated Charges"
    You can access all the active user-generated charges [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BJVvyJWaeHCCz-Onfc8wGJ1V6fLLX-dtQSwxb7xWbQY/edit?tab=t.bkm4depgwryb)!
The charges that can fall upon the parties in a trial are predominantly user-generated. However there are some preset charges that will never expire in usage. They're rudimentary charges that were meant to set a baseline and serve as a template for user-generated charges.

=== ":lucide-columns-4: Jail"
    The recipient of the Jail charge is given the <span class="mention">@incarcerated</span> role and is banished from all communication on the server. This excludes a chatroom and voice-chat, the <span class="mention">#cellblock</span> and <span class="mention">#showers</span>.
=== ":lucide-flag: Probation"
    Instead of being put into incarceration throughout their sentence, they're allowed to roam supervised by other members under the <span class="mention">@probation</span>. If they trip up, their charges will worsen and additional charges may pile on.
=== ":lucide-coins: Tax Audits"
    This is essential just a fine. A set amount of money, or a percentage is taken from the recipient's total networth with Tucker Isles's economy bot.
=== ":lucide-id-card: License Suspension"
    The recipient is effectively suspended from monetary gain, using economy channels, and the economy bot. 
=== ":lucide-cone: Dunce"
    The recipient gets a shameful role and is put onto display on the Member List menu. Anyone who has the <span class="mention">@dunce</span> role is considered a dolt, or a dullard.
=== ":lucide-id-card-lanyard: Ordered Nickname"
    Recipient is forced to wear a nickname decided by the judge and jury.
